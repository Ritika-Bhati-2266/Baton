How the workflow runs

  1. ConnectAuthorised sources are ingested read-only with an access label.
  2. ReconstructTyped claims are extracted with the exact supporting quote.
  3. VerifyA deterministic verifier checks every quote, then detects conflicts and gaps.
  4. ReviewA responsible person edits, rejects, resolves and approves per claim.
  5. HandoverApproved claims are filtered for the reader's role and published as a plan.
